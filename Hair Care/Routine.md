
### **Day 1: Wash Day** 

> [!NOTE] Products
> Hydrating Shampoo: SheaMoisture Raw Shea Butter Moisture Retention Shampoo
> Leave In Conditioner: Shea Moisture Castor Oil
> Mousse: 
> Oil: Coconut


- **Shampoo & Condition:**
    
    - Start with a hydrating shampoo focusing on your roots, followed by a deep conditioning treatment.
    - Use a shower brush to distribute conditioner and detangle your hair in sections.
    - Let the conditioner sit in your hair while twisted.
    - Rinse out the conditioner thoroughly.
- **Moisturize:**
    
    - Apply a leave-in conditioner followed by mousse.
    - Seal with a styling gel or curl cream and finish with a sealing oil.
- **Dry:**
    
    - Air dry or use a diffuser on low heat with a heat protectant.

### **Day 2: Refresh & Protect**

> [!NOTE] Products
> Leave In: Water Down Leave In
> Oil: Coconut
> 

- **Moisturize:**
    
    - Lightly spritz your hair with water or a water-based leave-in conditioner.
    - Reapply a light oil to seal in moisture if needed.
- **Style:**
    
    - Put your hair into a protective style (e.g., braids, twists, or a high bun).
    - Cover your hair with a satin or silk scarf or bonnet at night.

### **Day 3: Co-Wash & Detangle**

> [!NOTE] Products
> CoWash: As I AM cowash
> 

- **Co-Wash:**
    
    - Use a co-wash or cleansing conditioner to gently cleanse your hair without stripping it of moisture.
    - Detangle in the shower with a conditioner and wide-tooth comb.
- **Moisturize & Style:**
    
    - Repeat the moisturizing routine with a leave-in conditioner, styling product, and sealing oil.
    - Style as desired.

### **Day 4: Protective Styling**

- **Style:**
    
    - Re-style your hair into a low-manipulation style like a twist-out or braid-out.
    - Lightly moisturize if needed.
- **Protect:**
    
    - At night, cover your hair with a satin or silk bonnet or scarf.

### **Day 5: Light Moisturizing**

- **Refresh:**
    
    - Spritz your hair with water or a moisturizing spray.
    - Apply a small amount of leave-in conditioner or lightweight oil if needed.
- **Protect:**
    
    - Continue wearing your protective style, refreshing as necessary.

### **Day 6: Deep Condition**

- **Deep Conditioning Treatment:**
    
    - Apply a deep conditioner, focusing on the ends of your hair.
    - Use a shower cap to trap heat and help the conditioner penetrate your low porosity hair.
    - Rinse out thoroughly.
- **Moisturize & Style:**
    
    - Reapply your leave-in conditioner, styling product, and sealing oil.
    - Air dry or diffuse with a heat protectant.

### **Day 7: Rest & Protect**

- **Light Moisturizing:**
    
    - Refresh your hair with a moisturizing spray or light leave-in conditioner.
- **Protect:**
    
    - Keep your hair in a low-manipulation style.
    - Cover with a satin or silk scarf or bonnet at night.
