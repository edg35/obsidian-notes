### Core Definitions & Philosophy

- **The Hamming Quote**: "The purpose of computing is insight, not numbers."
- **Data Science (DS)**: The application of computational and statistical techniques to address or gain insights into real-world problems.
- **Interdisciplinary Nature**: DS sits at the intersection of **Hacking Skills**, **Math & Statistics Knowledge**, and **Substantive Expertise**.
    - **The "Danger Zone"**: Combining hacking skills and substantive expertise without math/stats knowledge.
        
- **Traditional Programming vs. Machine Learning**:
    - **Traditional**: Data + Program $\rightarrow$ Computer $\rightarrow$ Output.
    - **Machine Learning**: Data + Output $\rightarrow$ Computer $\rightarrow$ Program.

### The Data Science Life Cycle

A standard workflow for tackling data problems:

1. **Problem Definition**: Understanding objectives.
2. **Data Acquisition**: Gathering from web, APIs, sensors, etc.
3. **Data Cleaning**: Handling outliers, noise, and missing data.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Summarizing and visualizing patterns.
5. **Feature Engineering**: Transforming variables for modeling.
6. **Modeling**: Algorithm selection, training, and validation.
7. **Deployment**: Production monitoring and maintenance.
8. **Communication**: Storytelling and presenting results to stakeholders.

### Modern Drivers of Data Science

- **Data Volume**: We reached ~44 zettabytes of global data in 2020; Google alone processes 20+ petabytes daily.
- **Hardware**: Advances in CPU, GPU, TPU, and specialized ASICs (e.g., Cerebras, Groq).
- **Algorithms**: The rise of Deep Learning and Neural Networks.

### Key Concepts & Tools

- **Big Data**: Defined by the 3 Vs—**Volume, Velocity, and Variety**.
- **Machine Learning (ML)**: Includes Supervised, Unsupervised, and Reinforcement Learning.
- **Deep Learning**: Growing importance of Neural Networks, CNNs, LSTMs, and Transformers.
- **The Toolkit**:
    - **Languages**: Python, R.
    - **Databases**: SQL, NoSQL, Pinecone (VectorDB).
    - **EDA/Viz**: Jupyter, Matplotlib, Seaborn.
    - **ML Libraries**: Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, JAX.

### Challenges & Ethics: "Beyond Accuracy"

- **Overfitting**: When a model memorizes noise in training data instead of learning general patterns.
- **Security**: Models can be fooled by "Trojan attacks" (e.g., misidentifying a modified stop sign).
- **Privacy**: Risk of models remembering sensitive training data (e.g., facial recognition data).
- **Fairness**: Addressing biases in data that lead to accuracy disparities across different racial or gender groups.

### Famous Historical Examples

- **Survivorship Bias (WWII)**: Abraham Wald realized the military should armor areas _without_ bullet holes (engines/cockpit) on returning planes, as planes hit in those areas never made it back to be measured.
- **The Broad Street Pump (1854)**: Dr. John Snow used data mapping to prove cholera was water-borne, not airborne, by identifying a contaminated pump.
