# General Strategies

## 1. Divide and Conquer

- Break a problem into smaller sub problems, solve each, and combine for the solution
- Example [[Merge Sort]]

## 2. [[Greedy Algorithm]]

- Goal: make the best local choice at each step with the hope of finding a global optimum
- Greedy Approach: Always pick the activity that finishes the earliest

## 3. Dynamic Programming

- Break down a problem into overlapping subproblems, solving each just once and store their solution
